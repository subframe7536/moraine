## Features

- **Horizontal & Vertical Orientations**: Controls the line direction and sets matching `aria-orientation` metadata.
- **Border Styles**: Choose `solid`, `dashed`, or `dotted`.
- **Thickness Scale**: `sm`, `md`, and `lg` adjust the rendered separator thickness.
- **Semantic or Decorative Use**: The root always renders as `<div role="separator">`; `decorative` adds `aria-hidden="true"` so assistive technologies ignore it.
