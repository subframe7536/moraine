## Features

- **Horizontal & Vertical Orientations**: Automatically applies full width for horizontal rules or full height for inline vertical dividers.
- **Border Stroke Styles**: Choose from `solid`, `dashed`, and `dotted` lines.
- **Calibrated Thickness Scale**: Three thickness levels (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) matching theme border scales.
- **Accessible By Default**: Sets `role="none"` or `role="separator"` depending on `decorative` prop.

## Anatomy

Hierarchical slot and element structure:

```text
root (semantic <hr> or decorative divider element)
```

- `root`: Line element applying orientation, border style, and theme border colors (`border-border`).
