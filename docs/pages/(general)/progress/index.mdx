## Features

- **Determinate & Indeterminate Modes**: Displays precise percentage values or animated indeterminate pulses when duration is unknown.
- **4 Distinct Animation Styles**: Choose from `carousel`, `reverse`, `swing`, and `elastic` indeterminate visual physics.
- **Step Checkpoints**: Pass an array of step names (`['Draft', 'Review', 'Approved', 'Shipped']`) to render discrete progression dots and milestone labels.
- **Horizontal & Vertical Orientations**: Switch to vertical progress bars for column meters and audio level visualizers.
- **WAI-ARIA Progressbar Role**: Complies with `role="progressbar"`, providing `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, and `aria-valuemax`.

## Anatomy

Hierarchical slot and element structure:

```text
root (progress container)
├── status (determinate percentage or label, optional)
├── track (background bar rail)
│   └── indicator (animated fill bar)
└── steps (discrete milestone checkpoints, optional)
    └── step (individual step dot & label ×n)
```

- `root`: Outer layout element managing orientation and ARIA role.
- `status`: Text or custom renderer indicating numeric progress.
- `track`: The background track groove.
- `indicator`: Colored bar filling from 0% to 100%.
- `steps`: Step milestone ticks placed along the track.
