## Features

- **Determinate & Indeterminate Modes**: Provide a value for determinate progress or use an indeterminate animation when completion cannot be measured.
- **Animation Presets**: `carousel`, `reverse`, `swing`, and `elastic` provide built-in indeterminate motion styles.
- **Step Markers**: Supply step labels to display discrete milestones along the progress track.
- **Horizontal & Vertical Orientations**: Choose the track direction that fits the surrounding layout.
- **Progressbar Metadata**: Exposes `role="progressbar"` and value-related ARIA attributes for determinate progress.
